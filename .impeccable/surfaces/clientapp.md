# Rekr app — surface brief

Scope: the whole `clientApp` (splash, auth, both onboarding wizards, candidate feed, offer detail, recruiter offers, offer form, applicants, candidate detail, matches, match, profile). Mode: Operate.

Audience and job: candidates of every profile deciding on offers in seconds; TPE/PME recruiters publishing offers and reviewing interested candidates. Every feature, route and API contract stays as is.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The category standard, played straight and executed at Malt's level of finish. Refuses both the current mint/violet dual-role gradients and any metaphor world. Decisions read as aligned label/value rows, not piles of chips.

OWN-WORLD: Near-white cool grey ground (#f6f6f8), white cards 18px radius with a soft offset shadow, hairline #e5e7eb rows. One accent, ink violet #5b3fd6 (tint #eeeafd, text #4a31b8), used only on the primary action, active tab, selected filter/chip, links and the match badge. Manrope throughout, 800 for headings, tight tracking. Lucide icons. No gradients, no glass, no role colours.

STORY: The visitor sees who is hiring, what the job is, and the four facts that decide it (contract, rhythm, experience, salary) in one aligned block; they pass or say "Ça m'intéresse"; a match is announced calmly with a recap of the offer.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Feed: logo left and position counter right; one white card with the company row (initials avatar, name, size·city), a 20px title, four label/value rows, skill tags, a "Voir l'offre complète" link; below, two equal buttons, outline "Passer" and violet "Ça m'intéresse"; bottom tab bar with a violet underline on the active tab.

FORM: Canon (category standard), user's standing exit after the direction round; seed key b1f670f3.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
